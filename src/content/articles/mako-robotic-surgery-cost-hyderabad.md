# Understanding Mako Robotic Knee Surgery Cost

When patients discover the benefits of robotic joint replacement—specifically the sub-millimeter precision of the Stryker Mako 4.0 system—their next question is often regarding the financial investment. 

## Average Cost Range
In Hyderabad, a Mako Robotic Knee Replacement typically ranges from **₹2.8 Lakhs to ₹4.5 Lakhs** per knee. 

## What Dictates the Cost?

The variation in pricing depends on several critical factors:

1.  **Implant Choice:** Premium, imported implants (like the Stryker Triathlon system) designed to last 20+ years carry a higher upfront cost but offer superior longevity and biomechanics.
2.  **Hospital Infrastructure:** Surgery performed in a Class 100 Laminar Airflow theater (which virtually eliminates infection risk) with dedicated robotic hardware involves higher overhead than standard operating rooms.
3.  **Surgeon Expertise:** Procedures led by highly credentialed surgeons with international fellowships (like Dr. Amith Reddy) reflect top-tier clinical governance.
4.  **Length of Stay:** Robotic surgery often allows for a faster discharge (sometimes within 2-3 days), which can offset some room and board costs compared to traditional surgery.

## Is it Worth the Premium?

While robotic surgery may cost 15-20% more than a conventional replacement, the benefits often outweigh the initial expense:
*   **Lower Revision Rate:** Perfectly aligned implants wear out slower, reducing the need for a second surgery.
*   **Faster Return to Work:** Less tissue trauma means a quicker return to your livelihood.
*   **Reduced Physiotherapy Needs:** Many patients reach their mobility milestones faster, requiring fewer paid rehab sessions.
